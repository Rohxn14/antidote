## External test set: RippleEdits (structure only)

15 cases from RippleEdits (Cohen et al., TACL 2024; MIT licence), anonymised. Each case's edit is retracted in turn. Ground truth: what loses all support, computed from the dataset's own facts by the independent reference solver. The dataset labels 394 facts as following from the edits; 32 of them are also stated by the knowledge base (in 2 cases the edit restates a known fact), so a retraction must keep them.
